Privacy Policy

Last updated September 20, 2026

TowerDub does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data — full stop. Everything you create stays on your device.

Microphone access

TowerDub requests microphone access solely to record samples in the Sampler and to mix a live input through the Delay and Reverb effects. Any audio you record stays on your device — it is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared with anyone, including the developer.

Where your data lives

All your projects, patterns, sample recordings, and dub mixes are stored locally on your device. Nothing is synced to a server, because TowerDub doesn't have one.

Tracking & analytics

TowerDub has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking software, and no user accounts. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is being collected in the first place.
